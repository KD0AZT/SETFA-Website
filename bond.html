Potential Fire Station and Equipment Bond

The Aug. 4 election has concluded, and voters approved Southeast Thurston Fire Authority's bond measure.

We appreciate everyone who took the time to learn about the proposal, attend community presentations, ask questions, and participate in the election process. Thank you for your engagement and for helping shape the future of fire and emergency medical services in our community.

What Happens Next

Southeast Thurston Fire Authority will begin planning for several fire station renovations and the replacement of aging fire engines identified in the district's Capital Facilities Plan. These improvements will enhance firefighter health and safety while helping ensure our facilities and apparatus continue to support reliable emergency response throughout the district.

Planning, design, and project timelines will take time, and work will be completed in phases.

Staying Connected

We are committed to being responsible stewards of the public's investment an will continue to communicate with our community as these projects move forward. Updates will be shared on this website, through our social media channels, and at Board of Fire Commissionners meetings.

Thank you again for your participation in this important community decision.
